How this works

Outdoors = walking (straight-line distance × 1.3 at 80 m/min) plus waiting at the bus stop. For buses, the app tells you when to leave so you wait inside and arrive at the stop about 2 min early. Feels like = door-to-door minutes + 2 × outdoor minutes × (rain + haze + heat penalty) at each end of the trip. In fine weather it's just travel time.
